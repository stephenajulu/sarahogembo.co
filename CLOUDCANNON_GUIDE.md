# Sarah Ogembo Personal Brand Website
## CloudCannon CMS Owner Guide (No Technical or Coding Skills Required)

Welcome to your website management dashboard! This website has been built so that **you do not need any coding or technical knowledge** to update your content, add new photos, publish articles, or change your contact details.

Everything is editable through **CloudCannon CMS** using a visual, point-and-click editor.

---

### 1. How to Log In to CloudCannon

1. Go to [app.cloudcannon.com](https://app.cloudcannon.com/) in any web browser.
2. Log in using your email address and password.
3. Select your website project: **Sarah Ogembo (`sarahogembo.co`)**.

---

### 2. Visually Editing the Homepage

1. In the left navigation menu, click **Pages**.
2. Click **Home (`_index.md`)**.
3. You will see two powerful ways to edit:
   - **Visual Editor (On-Screen Editing):** On the right side of your screen, your real live website is displayed. Simply click directly on any headline, sentence, or button to start typing.
   - **Sidebar Form Editor:** On the left side of your screen, every section is neatly organized into clear, labeled form fields with simple inputs and photo pickers.
4. Click the green **Save** button in the top right corner when you are done. Your changes are saved and built automatically!

---

### 3. What You Can Edit on the Homepage

Here is an overview of the sections available in your editor:

#### A. Hero Section (Top Screen)
- **Hero Eyebrow:** Change the top role title (e.g. `CORPORATE MC • CONFERENCE MODERATOR • EVENT HOST`).
- **Headlines:** Edit both the main title and the italic gold accent text.
- **Narrative Subtitle:** Update the introductory paragraph.
- **Hero Stage Photo:** Click the image to choose a new photo from your media library or upload one directly from your computer/phone.
- **Gold Script Flourish:** Change the handwritten script text (e.g. *“Events People Remember”*).
- **Buttons:** Change button text and where they link.

#### B. Trusted by Leading Organisations
- **Client Logos:** Add new corporate partners, remove old ones, or change their industry categories (e.g. Safaricom, Absa, UNDP, Microsoft).
- **Client Strip Image:** Update or upload a new client logos strip.

#### C. About Sarah Section
- **About Portrait Photo:** Change the editorial photo in the About section with one click.
- **Personal Narrative:** Add, remove, or edit paragraphs about your purpose, philosophy, and approach.
- **Gold Script Flourish:** Edit the handwritten note (*“Same People. Better Conversations.”*).

#### D. Prestige Metrics (Stat Plaque)
- Edit your achievements (e.g. `250+ Events Hosted`, `50,000+ Audience Members Engaged`, `100% Commitment to Excellence`).
- Click **+ Add Item** to add a new statistic or drag to reorder.

#### E. Event Services
- Manage your 6 luxury service offerings (*Corporate Conferences, Executive Summits, Panel Moderation, Awards Galas, Product Launches, Corporate Celebrations*).
- Click on any service card to change its:
  - **Service Title**
  - **Display Icon** (choose from a simple dropdown menu: *Conference, Summit, Microphone, Trophy, Star, Calendar*)
  - **Description**
  - **Key Deliverable Outcomes** (add or remove bullet points)
- Click **+ Add Item** to introduce a new service anytime.

#### F. The Difference
- Edit the headline: *“It’s Not Just What I Say, It’s How I Make People Feel.”*
- Edit the 8-point signature checklist (e.g. *Genuine Connection, Calm Professionalism, Thoughtful Preparation, Grace Under Pressure*).

#### G. Video Showreel
- **Thumbnail Cover:** Choose the preview image displayed before the video plays.
- **Video Embed Link:** Simply paste any YouTube or Vimeo video link.
- **Caption & Description:** Update the text accompanying your reel.

#### H. Client Testimonials
- Manage your executive endorsements.
- For each testimonial:
  - Edit the **Client Quote**
  - Edit the **Author Name** (e.g. *Dr. Brenda Mutua*)
  - Edit their **Role** (e.g. *Corporate Communications Director*)
  - Edit their **Organization** (e.g. *Regional Financial Summit*)
  - Set the **Star Rating** (1 to 5 stars)
- Click **+ Add Item** to add a new testimonial from your latest conference.

#### I. Recent Moments (Photo Gallery)
- Easily showcase your latest stage appearances and moderation photos.
- For each photo:
  - **Image:** Upload the new photo directly from your computer.
  - **Title:** Event name (e.g. *Kenya Innovation Week Moderation*).
  - **Category:** Tag (e.g. *Summit, Keynote, Gala*).
  - **Caption:** One or two sentences describing the event and venue.
- Click **+ Add Item** to add more stage photos anytime.

#### J. Beyond The Stage (Personal Brand)
- Update your executive portrait.
- Edit your personal message on why human connection, communication, and mentorship matter.
- Update your 4 Core Pillars (*Executive Poise, Diligent Research, Authentic Warmth, Adaptive Mastery*).

#### K. Booking Concierge & Contact Details
- Update the invitation headline and subtext.
- Update the pre-filled WhatsApp text and booking button labels.

### 4. Updating Global Contact Info, Phone Numbers & Social Links

To update your contact information across the entire website at once:
1. In CloudCannon, go to **Data**.
2. Click **Settings (`settings.yml`)**.
3. You can edit:
   - **Official Email Address** (e.g. `hello@sarahogembo.com`)
   - **Direct Phone Number** (e.g. `+254 719 411 433`)
   - **WhatsApp Number** (for direct booking chats)
   - **LinkedIn Profile URL**
   - **Instagram Profile URL**
   - **Inspirational Footer Quote**
   - **Copyright Year**
4. Click **Save**. The header, contact section, and footer will update automatically.

---

### 5. Publishing Thought Leadership Articles (Blog)

Writing articles on event hosting, panel moderation, and leadership establishes immense executive authority with prospective clients.

To publish a new article:
1. In CloudCannon, click **Blog** in the left menu.
2. Click **+ Add Article** in the top right corner.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title:** (e.g. *5 Rules for Moderating High-Stakes Ministerial Panels*)
   - **Date:** Today's date
   - **Summary:** A 2-sentence preview of the article
   - **Tags:** e.g. *Panel Moderation, Executive Summits*
4. Type or paste your article into the visual rich-text editor (you can format bold text, add headings, blockquotes, and bullet points just like Microsoft Word or Google Docs).
5. Click **Save**. Your new article is published and automatically appears on `/blog/`!

---

### 6. Publishing Changes to the Live Website

- Whenever you click **Save**, CloudCannon saves your changes to your secure repository.
- Your website host (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or GitHub Pages) automatically detects the save and updates your live website in approximately 30 seconds.
- You never need to touch a terminal, write a single line of code, or call a developer!
